About Sentinel Range Works

Training with a clear standard.

Sentinel Range Works is built around professional firearms education, responsible handling, and a student experience that is organized, practical, and easy to understand.

Our approach

Safety, responsibility, and useful instruction.

SRW’s role is to train. That means keeping the focus on the material, the student, and the responsibilities that come with firearm ownership and use.

The website intentionally avoids claims about a physical range facility, memberships, pricing, or instructor biographies until those details are formally added by the business.

  • Professional course delivery
  • Safety-centered firearms education
  • Clear course completion documentation
  • Official Florida resources when applicable
The SRW standard

What students should be able to expect.

01 / PROFESSIONAL

Prepared instruction

Training should feel organized and purposeful from the first interaction through the final course documentation.

02 / RESPONSIBLE

Safety stays central

Firearms education starts with responsible behavior, careful handling, and respect for the responsibilities involved.

03 / PRACTICAL

Clear information

Students should leave understanding what they completed, what their documentation means, and what to do next when a license application is involved.
