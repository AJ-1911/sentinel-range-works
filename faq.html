Answers to the core questions around training certificates and the Florida concealed weapon or firearm license process.

No. Sentinel Range Works provides firearms training only. License applications and final approval are handled by the Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services.

For applicable SRW courses, students receive a course completion certificate for their records. When applicable, that certificate may be used as proof of firearms competency for a Florida Concealed Weapon or Firearm License application.

SRW recommends using the official FDACS application instructions and service-location resources. Regional licensing offices can be a convenient option because the application, photograph, and electronic fingerprints can generally be handled during the visit.
