About SRW

Professional firearms education without the noise.

Sentinel Range Works provides firearms training focused on preparation, confidence, and responsible ownership. The goal is simple: give students useful instruction, a professional training experience, and accurate documentation when the course is complete.

  • Structured, safety-focused instruction
